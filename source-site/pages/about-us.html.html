About Us

Home About Us

PRISMTECH PACKAGING SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD. is a leader in Manufacturing of Packaging Equipments, providing high-quality Packaging Equipments to customers across the globe. Established in 2002, our company has grown rapidly to become one of the most reputable, trusted, and reliable businesses in the industry.

At PRISMTECH PACKAGING SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD. , we specialize in providing Complete Solutions in Primary Packaging, Labelling, & End of the Line Equipments that meet the highest industry standards. Our focus on innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction has allowed us to build long-standing relationships with our clients, who trust us to deliver precisely what they need when they need it.

Our range of Primary Packaging, Labelling & End of the Line Equipments is extensive and includes.

  • Automatic Bottle Unscrambler
  • Automatic Bottle Cleaning Machines
  • Automatic Linear & Rotary Bottle Filling Machines
  • Automatic Linear & Rotary Plugging Machines
  • Automatic Linear & Rotary Capping Machines
  • Automatic Self-Adhesive Sticker Labelling Machines
  • Automatic Shrink Sleeve Applicators
  • Material Handling Equipments
  • Semi-Automatic Filling Machines
  • Semi-Automatic Capping Machine
  • Special Purpose Equipments for Packaging
  • Gantry Based or Robotic Case Erector & Case Packer
  • Robotic Palletizer
  • Batch Printers (We integrate with our Equipments)
